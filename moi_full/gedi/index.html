Rule

1. Pick an amount, then tap the food you want to bet on.

2. Each round has a betting time. When it ends the wheel spins and the result is shown at once. Betting is closed while it spins.

3. If the result matches the food you chose, you win your bet × the number shown on that food (x5, x10, x15, x25, x45).

4. You can bet on more than one food in the same round.

5. Sometimes the FRUIT basket comes out. Then all the fruits win together (x5).

6. Sometimes the SWEET platter comes out. Then all the sweets win together (x10, x15, x25, x45).

7. Rounds never stop — a new one starts by itself.
