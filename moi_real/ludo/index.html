Rule

1. Pick an amount, then tap the number you think the dice will show. You can use the numbers 2 to 6.

2. You can bet on up to 2 different numbers in the same round.

3. When the betting time ends, the dice rolls by itself. Betting is closed while it rolls.

4. If the dice stops on your number, you win your bet × that number. Example: 100 on number 3 and the dice shows 3 = 300.

5. The dice never shows 1.

6. Rounds never stop — a new one starts by itself.
